RouteGPT.

Getting across Dhaka starts with a question. RouteGPT turns natural language transport questions into guidance on routes, transfers, and fares.

01 / CONTEXTA city question, in everyday words.

A transport question often contains more than a destination. A passenger may need to know which route to take, where to change, or how much the journey costs. RouteGPT focuses on those practical questions in the context of Dhaka.

The project is a conversational assistant built with Python, natural language processing, and MySQL. Its interface accepts natural language questions, while the transport information lives in a relational database. This connects the way a person asks for help with the structured records needed to describe a journey.

02 / IMPLEMENTATIONConnect the route to the answer.

Keep transport information structured

The database contains routes, stops, fares, and discount categories. These are related parts of the same journey: a route connects stops, and its fare information helps explain the cost. MySQL provides the relational foundation for that information.

Account for the transfer

RouteGPT handles multi-leg journeys and identifies transfer points. That extends its scope beyond a single direct route. The answer can describe the connection between segments so a passenger can understand how the pieces of a trip fit together.

Include a short-distance alternative

The assistant also includes a rickshaw fare alternative for short distances. This reflects the practical question behind the route request: how to cover the trip, including journeys where a short local ride is relevant.

03 / TAKEAWAYA conversation grounded in data.

The project demonstrates the connection between language processing and database design. A useful conversational answer needs both: an understanding of the question and organized information about the transport system it describes.

RouteGPT brings several related concerns into one assistant: route lookup, transfer points, fare questions, and short-distance alternatives. The database gives these features a common foundation rather than treating every question as an isolated response.
